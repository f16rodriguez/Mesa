Same engine, same bots, same board

Rules from logic.js: the 6-6 opens the series, play goes counter-clockwise, capicúa pays 25, a tranque goes to the lower pips, first to 200. Moves from bot.ts, the bots in practice mode. Every tile lands where scene-layout.ts puts it in 3D, bending at 27 cm.

The people don't move yet

Players are single paintings here. In the prototype they become cut-out puppets with a few poses: idle, reach, slam, laugh, the chin-up greeting. Sounds are synthesized stand-ins. Phones are left out; this is only what the TV shows.

What the run caught

The near player hid a third of the board

In the first TV painting, the head of the player nearest the camera covered 31% of the felt. The table is now painted from a higher camera and nothing covers the felt. Rule for every future plate: the near player's head stays below the felt.
